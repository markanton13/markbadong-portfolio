## Distinguish law from project choices

- **Binding instruments:** RA 10173 and its IRR; applicable NPC circulars (including 2022-04, 2023-04, 2023-06, 2023-07 and 16-03). Their application depends on the facts and any amendments/current interpretation.
- **NPC guidance:** registration FAQs/exemption instructions, rights pages, breach-reporting instructions, PIA guidance and model clauses explain regulator processes or recommended practice; they do not replace the statute/circular or individualized legal advice.
- **Project policy proposals:** 90-day retention, an unchecked acknowledgement, three equivalent languages, local-only first rollout, high-confidence validation and anonymous receipt/token are design choices; they do not themselves establish a lawful basis or compliance.
- **Legal review required:** individual owner’s formal legal identity/jurisdiction and PIC applicability; purpose-specific §§12/13 bases; minors/SPI disposition; Individual Professional/DPO and registration status; Cloudflare Email Routing/Gmail processor and transfer safeguards; rights exceptions and breach reportability.
