# Ask Mark — Privacy Request Operations

**Status:** Proposed operating procedure for owner review. Documentation only; this does not activate visitor intake, create an automated reply, or establish legal compliance.
**Review date:** 2026-10-23
**Official public contact:** [privacy@markbadong.com](mailto:privacy@markbadong.com)

## 1. Purpose and operating assumptions

Ask Mark is an individually operated professional portfolio project. The owner has designated `privacy@markbadong.com` as the public privacy contact and reports that Cloudflare Email Routing forwards incoming requests to a private Gmail inbox; the owner confirms an end-to-end delivery test succeeded. This document relies on that owner-provided description. It does not inspect the mailbox or independently verify service settings, account security, vendor terms, or ongoing delivery.

Use this procedure only after the owner has approved the applicable processing purposes and lawful bases, notice, retention schedule, processor arrangements, DPO/registration assessment, and tested deletion path. Keep public visitor intake disabled until those gates are satisfied. A privacy request may itself contain personal or sensitive personal information (SPI); treat the request mailbox and its copies as a separate personal-data flow.

## 2. Mailbox security and monitoring

Before publishing the address as an active request channel, the owner should:

- Assign one accountable mailbox operator and a named backup/coverage plan; check the inbox on each business day and record missed-coverage handling. This is a proposed operational cadence, not a legal deadline.
- Use a unique strong password and phishing-resistant passkey or multi-factor authentication. Protect recovery email/phone and offline recovery codes to an equivalent standard; review active sessions, connected apps, delegated access, and account alerts at least quarterly and after suspected compromise.
- Use a supported, encrypted, patched device with automatic screen lock, full-disk encryption, malware protection, and a separate browser profile or account context for privacy operations where practical. Do not use shared/public devices or public Wi-Fi without a trusted secure connection.
- Restrict mailbox access to the owner and specifically authorized backup only. Do not grant access to general portfolio tools, contractors, code repositories, issue trackers, or moderation accounts. Do not use consumer add-ons or AI email-reading integrations for requests.
- Disable lock-screen message previews and avoid notifications that expose request content. Do not configure Gmail to forward privacy requests to any further destination, auto-reply, or include request text in calendar/task notifications. This does not change the owner-reported Cloudflare Email Routing delivery into Gmail.
- Confirm account recovery and incident contacts remain usable without exposing recovery details in this repository. Review Gmail and Cloudflare security activity and alert promptly on unexpected access.
- Review the actual Cloudflare Email Routing and Gmail terms, processing roles, subprocessors, data locations/transfers, retention, deletion, access, and incident-notification commitments. An owner-reported delivery test proves delivery at that time, not security or contractual suitability.

These are recommended controls for this project. Select controls proportionate to the risk and applicable NPC requirements after qualified review.

## 3. Gmail organization and minimal tracking

Create a dedicated Gmail label such as **Ask Mark — Privacy Requests**. Apply it manually or through a narrow filter that matches only mail addressed to the public privacy alias. Do not route messages into unrelated labels or issue systems, and do not create an automated reply. Keep the message in the restricted mailbox until the retention decision is approved; avoid copying its full contents or attachments elsewhere.

For operational tracking, use a restricted, encrypted record separate from visitor intake, moderation, analytics, and public site code. Prefer a randomly generated case reference. Record only what is needed to manage the request:

- case reference and received/acknowledged/closed dates;
- request type and status;
- opaque submission ID, if supplied, and the minimum locator used;
- verification method/result (not copies of identity documents);
- decision, applicable legal exception or reason, action and verification result;
- response/extension date and responsible operator.

Do not record raw message text, unnecessary sender addresses, government-ID numbers/images, passwords, tokens, or free-form sensitive details in the tracker. Avoid duplicate notes. Approve a separate retention schedule for email, attachments, case metadata, and any evidence before use; delete case material when its documented purpose and any applicable legal hold end. Do not retain visitor data solely in anticipation of future access or portability requests. Ensure that Gmail trash, spam, exports, local downloads, and backups are included in deletion planning.

## 4. Receipt, triage, and acknowledgment

1. Open the request only in the protected mailbox. Do not copy it into application logs, D1, moderation notes, GitHub, or shared work tools.
2. Assign a random case reference and record the minimum tracker fields above. Treat credentials, medical/financial details, government identifiers, information about minors, or other likely SPI as restricted. Do not circulate the content to obtain informal advice.
3. Identify the requested right and whether the message appears to concern Ask Mark. Acknowledge manually using the template below. Do not promise a response from Mark to a portfolio question; this is a privacy-rights process, not a concierge-response guarantee.
4. If the request is ambiguous, ask only for the minimum non-sensitive detail needed to locate and understand it. Suggest that the requester not send passwords, full identity documents, or additional sensitive content by ordinary email.
5. Start the applicable response clock and retain evidence of the date received and any date necessary supporting information was received. The NPC's 2021-01 advisory says requests should be handled without undue delay, generally no later than 30 working days after receipt and/or necessary supporting or additional documentation; where complex or numerous, it describes an extension of up to 15 working days with notice of the reason. This is NPC guidance, not a deadline written verbatim in RA 10173. Re-check for later issuances and obtain qualified advice on applicability and computation before adopting the tracker. Do not wait until the outer limit if a request can reasonably be resolved earlier.
6. If the request is complex, numerous, raises an exception, or cannot be completed on time, notify the requester of the reason and expected next step within the applicable period; obtain qualified advice before relying on an extension.

The public address alone, or possession of a sender address, does not establish that the requester owns an anonymous submission. Do not confirm a record exists or disclose its contents until the request is matched and the requester is proportionately verified.

## 5. Verification and request handling

### Proportionate verification

- Prefer a random submission receipt/opaque ID retained by the visitor. A possession check can help link the request to a submission but is not conclusive identity proof.
- If no receipt exists, ask for minimal non-sensitive context such as an approximate submission date and a short locator that does not repeat the message. Use context only to find the record; do not disclose content based on a guess.
- Where the request concerns an identifiable individual and risk warrants further assurance, explain why, consider a reply through the same channel or another proportionate method, and request the least additional information reasonably needed. Avoid government IDs by default. Do not solicit an ID image or retain one unless qualified counsel determines it is necessary and a secure channel, handling rule, and immediate deletion plan are approved.
- For an authorized representative or transmissible right, obtain only proportionate evidence of authority under the applicable law. Escalate uncertainty; do not improvise identity requirements.
- If identity, authority, or record ownership cannot be reasonably verified, pause disclosure and irreversible action. Reply without confirming whether a particular record exists, explain what minimum additional proof or locator would help, offer a safe alternative where possible, and record the reason. Do not deny the person’s rights solely because the service was designed to be anonymous; get legal review if a request remains unresolved.

### Access

Search only the approved private systems and records within the request’s scope, including relevant intake, moderation/audit, export, log, email, or processor copies as applicable. Check for third-party data, security risks, privilege, and statutory limitations. Provide only the requester’s data and required information, via a reviewed safe channel. Do not expose internal credentials, other visitors’ data, or private moderation information unrelated to the requester.

### Correction / rectification

Determine whether the data is inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or otherwise correctable and verify the matching record. Apply only an authorized change; retain the minimum audit evidence needed to show what changed and when, without preserving the old sensitive content in an immutable note. Consider whether an update must be communicated to recipients, subject to legal review.

### Objection

Identify the processing purpose and legal basis, assess the objection and any applicable statutory ground to continue, and stop or limit the relevant processing where required. Escalate a dispute about direct marketing, consent withdrawal, legitimate interests, or other legal grounds. Do not treat objection as an automatic request to publish, erase, or alter unrelated records.

### Erasure / blocking

Assess the statutory grounds and exceptions before acting. If approved, use a tested runbook covering primary D1 rows, linked moderation actions/events, audit records, exports, logs, email copies, provider copies, backups, replicas, and restore-time re-deletion. Preserve only a minimal non-content audit/tombstone where counsel confirms a lawful purpose; avoid immutable retention of the message or secrets. Verify the deletion, document unavoidable expiry or exception accurately, and never promise complete immediate deletion if a copy or backup remains.

For each right, have the accountable PIC/operator or qualified privacy/legal reviewer decide whether it applies and whether an exception is available. Classification, moderation status, and publication approval are not proxies for a rights decision.

## 6. Templates for manual use

These drafts are not automated replies. Personalize only with approved minimum information; do not paste a visitor’s full message or sensitive content into a response.

### Acknowledgment

> Subject: We received your Ask Mark privacy request
>
> Hello,
>
> We received your privacy request on [date] and assigned reference [case reference]. We will review what you asked for and may contact you if a small amount of additional information is needed to locate or safely verify the relevant record. Please do not send passwords, government-ID images, or additional sensitive information by email.
>
> We will respond as soon as reasonably possible and within the applicable period. If the request is complex or requires more time, we will explain the reason and next step. This acknowledgment does not confirm that a particular submission exists or that the request has been granted.
>
> Ask Mark Privacy Contact

### Request for limited information or verification

> Subject: A little more information is needed for your privacy request
>
> Hello,
>
> To locate and protect the information related to your request, please provide [specific minimum non-sensitive locator or verification step] through [approved channel]. Please do not send a password, full government ID, or sensitive details. We will not disclose personal information until we can reasonably match the request and protect other people’s information.
>
> If you cannot provide this information, reply and we can consider another proportionate way to handle your request. We will explain any effect on timing.
>
> Ask Mark Privacy Contact

### Resolution / completion

> Subject: Update on your Ask Mark privacy request
>
> Hello,
>
> We completed our review of request [case reference] on [date]. [State the action taken or the information provided in plain language, without including unrelated personal data.] [If any copy remains temporarily or any part could not be completed, state what remains, why, the applicable exception or verified limitation, and the expected next review/delete date.]
>
> For questions about this request, contact privacy@markbadong.com and include reference [case reference].
>
> Ask Mark Privacy Contact

### Refusal or partial refusal

> Subject: Update on your Ask Mark privacy request
>
> Hello,
>
> We reviewed request [case reference]. We could not [state only the affected part] because [plain-language, legally reviewed reason or applicable limitation]. [State what can be done, any information still needed, and any review or complaint route that applies.] We have not disclosed another person’s information.
>
> If you believe we misunderstood the request, reply to this email or contact privacy@markbadong.com with reference [case reference]. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission using its current official process.
>
> Ask Mark Privacy Contact

Do not use a refusal template until the legal basis/exception and notice content have been reviewed for the specific request. Do not state a statutory deadline, NPC complaint procedure, or right as absolute without confirming current official requirements.

## 7. Suspected incident and escalation

Treat unexpected account access, misdirected requests, exposed attachments, lost devices, unauthorized disclosure, suspicious forwarding rules, mailbox lockout, or an uncontrolled copy as an incident candidate. Immediately:

1. Protect people and stop ongoing unauthorized access using the account provider’s safe controls. Preserve relevant timestamps and limited evidence; do not forward or broadly copy request content.
2. Notify the accountable PIC/operator and privacy/security adviser as soon as possible. Contact Cloudflare/Gmail through verified provider support channels as appropriate, without including unnecessary message content.
3. Record discovery time and facts, systems, data classes, affected people estimate, recipients/access, containment, and evidence source in a restricted incident record. Do not put secrets or visitor content in GitHub or ordinary logs.
4. Have qualified counsel/DPO assess whether a personal data breach occurred and whether all mandatory criteria apply. NPC guidance states that mandatory notification is not required for every event; where the notification criteria are met, NPC and affected-subject notification is due within 72 hours from knowledge or reasonable belief of the breach. The NPC page also states that an initial notification may use available information, with a full report due within five days unless more time is granted. Follow the current Data Breach Notification Management System and current Circular 16-03/related issuance; do not wait for full forensic certainty if the threshold is met.
5. Document the decision even if the event is not notifiable and follow current annual security incident reporting requirements. Escalate immediately if there is SPI, identity-fraud-enabling data, vulnerable people, a large affected group, ongoing exposure, or uncertainty about the clock.

This procedure does not itself file notices or authorize disclosure. Current notification criteria, channel, required contents, and any permitted delay/exemption must be confirmed at incident time with qualified advice and official NPC instructions.

## 8. Remaining DPA and release gates

The following remain unresolved; this procedure is not a declaration of compliance:

- **PIC and lawful bases:** Confirm the individual owner’s legal identity/jurisdiction and PIC role. Determine a documented RA 10173 §12 basis per purpose (live query and edge logs; optional intake; abuse/security metadata; moderation/audit; privacy requests) and a factually applicable §13 exception before processing SPI. A privacy notice or unchecked acknowledgment is not itself a lawful basis.
- **Missed sensitive-content detection:** Visitors may still submit SPI, credentials, and contact data. Approve a restricted triage, no-unnecessary-use, legal assessment, incident, redaction/deletion and verified backup/log cleanup path. Do not promise validation prevents such content.
- **DPO and registration:** Obtain qualified assessment of whether the self-employed professional meets NPC Circular 2022-04’s “Individual Professional” category (including de facto DPO treatment) and whether DPO/DPS registration, risk-triggered registration, or the required exemption declaration applies. Individual operation alone is not an exemption. Keep required identity/registration data out of source control.
- **PIA and governance:** The compliance document is a preliminary engineering PIA, not a signed owner-approved final assessment. Complete a proportionate PIA/control framework, assign accountability, and review when purposes, data, processors, or risks change.
- **Retention and deletion:** Approve class-specific periods for message, deduplication and requester bucket values, moderation notes/audit, rate/attempt counters, request email/case records, application/platform logs, backups and exports. Test purge, provider deletion, backup expiry and restore-time re-deletion before intake.
- **Processors and transfers:** Review Cloudflare and Gmail terms, roles, subprocessors, support access, locations/transfers, retention/deletion, security, and breach cooperation. The owner’s delivery test does not establish these safeguards.
- **Rights operations:** Assign mailbox operator/backup, daily-business-day monitoring, secure tracking location, record-retention period, verification standard, approved response text, and current response-deadline calculation. Publish a reviewed notice in accessible equivalent English, Filipino, and Taglish.
- **Incident readiness:** Name an incident lead/backup, secure evidence path and contacts, test detection/escalation, and confirm the currently applicable 72-hour and annual-report rules. No public intake until the privacy and technical gates are signed off.
